CLIMBING TYPES

DIFFERENT WALLS.
DIFFERENT MOVES.

Every climb combines technique and decision-making. These common types show how the wall changes the challenge.

01 / BODY TENSION

OVERHANG

Overhanging walls lean toward you. Keeping your hips close and using your feet well helps you avoid swinging away from the wall.

These problems often reward controlled pulling movements and strong body positioning.

02 / TIMING

DYNAMIC MOVEMENT

Some climbing problems require a coordinated movement between holds. The goal is to move with control and choose the right timing.

Route reading helps you decide when a static movement or a coordinated movement makes sense.

03 / PRECISION

TECHNICAL CLIMBING

Technical routes emphasize quiet feet, accurate positioning, and balance. A small shift in body position often changes how difficult a move feels.

Slab climbing, on walls that lean away from you, often puts these skills at the centre of the challenge.
