BEFORE YOU CLIMB

FOUR STARTER TIPS.

01

Wear climbing shoes

Many gyms rent shoes. Their grip and shape help with precise foot placement.

02

Start with easy climbs

Use beginner problems to learn the hold colours, wall rules, and basic movement.

03

Learn safe descents

Follow the gym's instructions, downclimb when appropriate, and learn how to use the padded landing area.

04

Take time to rest

Pause between attempts and observe how other climbers move through a problem.

NEXT STEP

READ THE ROUTE.
THEN MOVE.

Before starting, identify the starting holds, the next few movements, and the finish. Look for footholds that support your balance. After an attempt, decide what worked and what you want to change.

Practice controlled movements and use the gym's safety briefing as your guide.
