THE BASICS

SMALL WALL.
BIG CHALLENGE.

Bouldering is a type of climbing done on shorter walls without ropes. Indoor bouldering gyms provide padded landing surfaces and routes made from coloured handholds and footholds.

Each route is called a problem . You start at marked holds and work through a sequence of movements until you reach the finish. The challenge is to discover a sequence that works with your height, balance, and technique.

Unlike rope climbing, bouldering usually involves short attempts followed by rest and another try. It is common to test several ideas on the same problem.

WHY PEOPLE ENJOY IT

MORE THAN STRENGTH.

01

Problem solving

Reading a route encourages planning, trying different moves, and learning from each attempt.

02

Body control

Precise footwork and balance help you move efficiently without relying only on your arms.

03

Community

Climbers often share ideas and encourage one another while working on the same problems.

CLIMBING LANGUAGE

KNOW THE TERMS.

Problem

A short climbing route with a defined start and finish.

Beta

Advice about the movements that help complete a problem.

Crux

The hardest move or sequence in a route.

Top

The finishing hold or finishing position of a problem.
